EXPECTED OUTCOMES

Comprehensive Audit Results

Our objective is not to value the company or make an investment recommendation, but to determine whether the information provided by the company is complete, internally consistent, and ready for investor review.

Cross-Document Verification
Extract a defined set of financial and operational metrics and compare them across the pitch deck, financial statements, MIS, projections, and cap table.
Discrepancy Identification
Flag conflicting numbers, unsupported claims, missing information, and unusual changes in assumptions.
Readiness Reporting
Generate a source-linked inconsistency report, a list of specific follow-up questions, and a one-page fundraising readiness summary.
Classification Intelligence
Clearly distinguish between missing information, an unresolved inconsistency, and a verified mismatch.
